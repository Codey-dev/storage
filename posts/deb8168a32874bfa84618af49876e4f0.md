# Master Replication Prompt — Series Style DNA

> **Purpose:** Feed this prompt (or sections of it) into your image generation model to replicate the analyzed webcomic/sticker art style with maximum fidelity.
> **How to use:** The top block is the **immutable style DNA** — always include it. The bottom blocks are **character-specific variables** — swap them per illustration.

---

## PART A: IMMUTABLE STYLE DNA (Always Include)

### Core Style Lock
```
2D vector-clean webcomic illustration, highly stylized chibi/super-deformed proportions (head-to-body ratio ~1:1 up to shoulders), 
flattened parallel projection with minimal perspective, soft high-key RGB palette, pastel analogous color harmony, 
flat cel-shading with soft gradient shadows, thick uniform dark-indigo linework (~3-5px, #1A1A24, never pure black), 
matte surface finish, zero photorealism, zero painterly brush texture, optimized for 72 DPI screen display.
```

### Line Work Rules (Hard Constraints)
```
- Line weight: Uniform 3-5px across ALL elements. Do NOT vary by depth or object size.
- Line color: Dark indigo/charcoal (#1A1A24). NEVER pure black (#000000).
- Line quality: Crisp but with subtle organic wobble — hand-drawn feel, not mathematically vector-straight.
- Contours: Universal outlines around every form. No open contours.
- Hatching: NONE. No cross-hatching except for stylized panic pupils (cross-hatched circles).
- Stroke direction: Lines follow object contours (wrap around jaw curves, flow with hair fall).
```

### Color System Rules (Hard Constraints)
```
- Primary palette: Pastel Pink (#F8C8D4), Blonde (#F0D7B9), Dark Indigo/Purple (#5B5485), Muted Purple (#A48AAD), Brown (#7C6B5E).
- Secondary: Light Beige skin (#FFEFE6), White (#FFFFFF).
- Accents: Deep Red clothing (#B32D3A), Sparkling Gold (#FEEAA1).
- Harmony: Analogous, occasionally triadic (Purple/Blue + Yellow/Gold accents).
- Temperature: Warm dominant (pinks, beiges, reds) contrasted against cool backgrounds (teal/mint, purple/blue).
- Saturation: Selectively muted. Skin is pale/desaturated. Hair and clothes hold the most saturation.
- Value range: High-key (values 7-10). Darkest darks only in line work and deep clothing shadows (values 1-3).
- Shadows: Darker, desaturated tints of the LOCAL object color. NO optical grays. NO cast shadows. 
  Only core/form shadows on jawline underside, hair folds, armpits/clothing folds.
- Highlights: Diffuse soft ovals on hair only. Skin has NO specular highlights or catchlights. 
  Eyes are pure white or flat colored — NO realistic iris detail, NO catchlights.
- Transitions: Flat color blocks for clothing/hair. Soft blended gradients for skin tone only.
- NO dithering. NO texture overlays. NO noise.
```

### Lighting Rules (Hard Constraints)
```
- Light source: Single diffuse soft overhead light.
- Light quality: Soft. NO hard-edged geometric shadows.
- Shadow type: Core shadows only (form-wrapping). NO cast shadows.
- Ambient occlusion: Minimal. Deep fold shadow where chin meets neck, hair meets face.
- Rim light: ONLY in "sticker" mode — white contour outline acts as rim light.
- NO subsurface scattering. NO environmental reflections. NO wet/glossy surfaces.
```

### Shape Language (Hard Constraints)
```
- Primary shapes: Circles and ovals ONLY. Soft, round, friendly, harmless.
- Hair: Drawn as smooth, singular clumps (not individual strands). Bubbly, rounded silhouettes.
- Face: Massive rounded head, enormous oval eyes, minimal or absent nose, small simplified mouth.
- Hands: Small, simplified to 3-4 thick fingers. No detailed anatomy.
- Silhouette: Must be 10/10 readable at pure black. Every element distinct.
- Form rendering: Flat color + minimal soft shading. NO chiaroscuro. NO dramatic 3D modeling.
```

### Anatomy & Proportions (Hard Constraints)
```
- Proportion style: Chibi / Super-Deformed.
- Head-to-body: ~1:1 ratio (head as large as body down to shoulders/chest).
- Eyes: Enormous, dominant facial feature. Blank white circles, black pin-dots, horizontal slits, 
  or cross-hatched panic pupils. NEVER anatomically correct irises.
- Nose: Minimal or absent.
- Mouth: Small, expressive, comedic.
- Body: Cropped at shoulders/chest. Character bleeds off bottom edge.
- Perspective: Flattened/parallel. Character faces viewer with minor three-quarter turns.
```

### Background Rules
```
- Background type: Flat single color, OR soft blurred bokeh (simulated shallow depth of field), 
  OR cosmic/galaxy gradient, OR soft pastel wash.
- NO detailed environments. NO complex architecture. NO textured ground planes.
- Background colors: Cool muted tones (teal, mint, purple, blue) to contrast warm character.
- Atmospheric perspective: Minimal. Background may be slightly cooler/muted than foreground.
```

### Graphic Treatment (Sticker Mode — Use When Applicable)
```
- When creating sticker/emote style: Add heavy white outline (3-5pt stroke) around entire character silhouette.
- White outline acts as rim light against background.
- Character fully enclosed within white border, isolated from background like a peel-off decal.
- Background can be transparent or solid color.
```

### Atmosphere & Effects (Use Sparingly)
```
- Allowed effects: Floating golden dust motes, bright white four-pointed sparkles (for satisfaction/well-being), 
  soft god rays represented by sparkles, starry/galaxy background.
- NO weather effects (rain, snow, fog). NO lens flare. NO vignetting.
- Depth of field: ONLY as background blur (bokeh) to separate character. Character must remain sharp.
```

### Typography (If Including Text)
```
- Font style: Bold sans-serif comic font (resembling "Anime Ace" or "CC Wildwords").
- Text color: Black (#1A1A1A).
- Bubble: White fill, black outline, rounded edges.
- Integration: Overlaid standard comic speech bubble. NOT in-world signage.
```

## PART F: QUICK REFERENCE — DO vs. DON'T

| ✅ DO | ❌ DON'T |
|-------|----------|
| Uniform 3-5px dark indigo lines | Vary line weight by depth |
| Flat color blocks + soft skin gradients | Heavy shading or chiaroscuro |
| Core shadows only (local color tints) | Cast shadows or optical grays |
| Blank / stylized eyes (slits, dots, cross-hatch) | Realistic irises or catchlights |
| Simplified 3-4 finger hands | Detailed 5-finger anatomical hands |
| Cropped at chest, bleed off bottom | Full body or complex poses |
| Flat/minimal backgrounds | Detailed environments |
| Matte surfaces | Glossy, wet, or reflective surfaces |
| White sticker outline (when applicable) | Drop shadows or glow effects |
| Pastel analogous warm-dominant palette | Clashing or highly saturated skin |
